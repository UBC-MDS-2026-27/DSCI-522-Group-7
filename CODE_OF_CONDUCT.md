# Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone involved in the DSCI 522 Group 7 project. We value diversity and respect all contributors regardless of their background, identity, experience, or abilities.

## 2. Expected Behaviour

All contributors are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and use inclusive language.
- Respect different opinions and perspectives.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work collaboratively.
- Treat others with kindness and professionalism.

## 3. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or bullying.
- Offensive comments, personal attacks, or insulting language.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or inappropriate imagery.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Any other behaviour that creates an unsafe or disrespectful environment.

## 4. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related interactions, including GitHub issues, pull requests, code reviews, meetings, and other communication channels used by the team.

## 5. Reporting Violations

Anyone who experiences or witnesses unacceptable behaviour can report the incident to the project maintainers at **[info.dataforchange@gmail.com]**.

Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and confidentially. The maintainers will respect the privacy of everyone involved and take appropriate action.

## 6. Enforcement and Consequences

Depending on the severity of the violation, project maintainers may take the following actions:

1. **Correction:** Explain the inappropriate behaviour and request that it be corrected.
2. **Warning:** Issue a formal warning and explain the consequences of repeated violations.
3. **Temporary restriction:** Temporarily restrict participation in project activities.
4. **Permanent removal:** Remove a contributor from project participation in cases of serious or repeated violations, subject to applicable course and GitHub permissions.
